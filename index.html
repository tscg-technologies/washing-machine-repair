ELECTRICAL & CONTROLS ISSUE 01

Machine Not Turning On

When the power button is pressed, the appliance shows no display indicators, lights or audible signals, remaining completely unresponsive.

Broad Categories of Possible Causes:

Common broad categories include power supply interruption, wall socket/breaker issues, control board failure, or safety interlock faults.

Safe Home Observation Step:

Safely inspect whether the wall switch is securely connected and that other small devices work on that socket. Do not touch wet cables.
